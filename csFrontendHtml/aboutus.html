WHO WE ARE

About CSRoot

CSRoot is a modern web-based learning platform primarily designed to strengthen students' understanding of Computer Science Core Subjects.

Unlike platforms that mainly focus on coding practice, CSRoot gives equal importance to theoretical concepts that form the foundation of Computer Science.

The platform brings learning resources, practice questions, coding activities, assessments and collaborative learning together in one place.

Our Purpose

To bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical coding skills by providing students with a structured and engaging learning ecosystem.

Learn Understand core Computer Science concepts.
Practice Improve skills through questions and coding.
Apply Use knowledge through practical problem solving.
